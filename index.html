Builder Journey

Builder Journey

My journey into product development didn't begin with software.

It began with solving operational problems while helping build a restaurant business.

What started as using AI for documentation, systems, and process improvement gradually evolved into learning how products are designed, built, tested, and improved.

01
↗ Stage 1

Operations & Problem Solving

While helping build Yume Ramen, I started using AI to solve practical operational challenges.

Instead of spending hours creating documents manually, I began building systems for:

  • Food safety documentation
  • Allergen information
  • Pricing tools
  • Operational workflows
  • Process documentation

This was my first experience using AI as a practical business tool.

02
⟳ Stage 2

Discovering Automation

The more I used AI, the more curious I became about automation.

What started as creating documents evolved into questions like:

Can this process be automated?

Can this become a tool?

Could software solve this problem more effectively?

This was the point where I became interested in how products were built.

03
◇ Stage 3

Building Something I Wanted To Use

As someone interested in meditation, mental performance, and personal development, I wanted a more structured approach to mental training.

Instead of waiting for someone else to build it, I decided to try creating it myself.

This became VEXIS.

Through the project I began learning:

  • GitHub workflows
  • Product design
  • Deployment
  • Audio systems
  • Testing and iteration
  • AI-assisted development
04
✓ Stage 4

Learning Through Shipping

Building products introduced a completely new set of challenges.

Features broke. Audio failed. Deployments failed. Bugs appeared. Fixes created new bugs.

Through repeated iteration I learned how products evolve through testing, troubleshooting, and improvement.

Many of the skills I have today were learned while actively building and shipping projects.

05
＋ Stage 5

Today

Today I continue to build products while expanding my understanding of:

  • Product development
  • AI-assisted workflows

  • Deployment
  • APIs

Flagship Case Study

VEXIS

AI-Assisted Mental Performance Platform

VEXIS is a mental performance platform built around Awareness → Intuition → Execution.

What began as a simple guided meditation project evolved into a deeper exploration of mental training, product development, and AI-assisted creation.

Origin

Why VEXIS Exists

In 2020, I went through one of the most stressful periods of my life.

That experience led me to discover meditation through apps like Headspace and sparked a long-term interest in awareness, focus, performance, and human behaviour.

Over time I explored books, documentaries, mindfulness practices, and performance systems used by athletes, executives, and other high performers.

Years later, while building Yume Ramen and experimenting with AI tools, I found myself asking a simple question:

Could mental training become more accessible?

That question became the foundation of VEXIS.

First version

How It Started

The first version of VEXIS focused on guided audio meditation practices designed to help users learn the foundations of mental training. There was no structured progression, behavioural intelligence, or SignAI yet — just a practical training tool and a commitment to learn through building.

Product evolution

How VEXIS Evolved

01

Guided Audio Practices

VEXIS moved from static practice instructions into an active session experience with a clear focus object, playback state, progress, and reflection loop.

02

Structured Training

The product then introduced a clearer training map, grouping practices into foundation, intuition, and flow so users could understand where they were in the journey.

03

Progress Tracking

Progress tracking made the experience feel more accountable by showing sessions completed, minutes trained, streaks, completion rate, and recent history.

04

Expanded Practice Library

Completion states and next-practice flows supported a broader practice library by helping users finish, repeat, or continue through the training sequence.

05

SignAI Vision

The final concept layer explored how behavioural signals could one day inform adaptive recommendations, training paths, and personalised scripts.
